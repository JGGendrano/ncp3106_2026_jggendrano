About SCpES

The Center of Student Innovation

The Society of Computer Engineering Students (SCpES) serves as the official academic, technical, and socio-civic community of Computer Engineering students at the University of the East. SCpES bridges theoretical coursework with hands-on practice, giving students platforms for leadership, technical skill development, peer tutorials, and direct industry networking.

#EngineeredForExcellence #PoweredToEngineer #AllOutCPE

Organization Objectives

The primary goals that steer all SCpES programs and student initiatives.

Academic Guidance

Conducting student-led tutorials, reviewer drives, and review workshops for core subjects including logic circuits, microprocessors, and mathematics.

Technical Mastery

Equipping students with modern industry capabilities through hands-on bootcamps in microcontrollers, IoT sensor telemetry, firmware, and software development.

External Linkages

Cultivating active partnerships with tech companies, engineering alumni practitioners, and the Institute of Computer Engineers of the Philippines (ICpEP).
