BSCpE Department Spotlight

Computer Engineering at UE

Introducing the BS Computer Engineering program at the University of the East across Manila and Caloocan campuses.

Objectives & Skills Projects & Research

Overview of the Program

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering (BSCpE) program at the University of the East integrates computer science theory with electronic engineering principles to design, build, and optimize hardware-software systems.

Delivered through Outcome-Based Education (OBE) across both UE Manila and UE Caloocan campuses, the curriculum provides balanced expertise in embedded systems, computer architecture, network infrastructure, digital logic design, and system-level software integration.

Accredited Curriculum
Aligned with outcome-based engineering education frameworks and industry requirements.
Dual Campus Provision
Fully equipped academic environments at both UE Manila and UE Caloocan campuses.

Objectives & Core Competencies

Structuring professional competence and hands-on skill development.

Program Objectives

  • Equip graduates with technical mastery in hardware architecture and software engineering.
  • Foster professional ethics, leadership qualities, and commitment to lifelong learning.
  • Prepare students for complex problem-solving through innovative research and system development.
  • Align student training with evolving global technology industries and certification pathways.

Skills Developed

Microprocessor Design Low-level Assembly (8085/8086) C / C++ / Python FPGA Prototyping Network Routing & Switching Digital Logic & Circuit Simulation IoT System Integration OS Architecture & Scheduling

Students build technical expertise spanning circuit-level design, embedded microcontroller programming, operating systems, and enterprise software systems.

Learning Experiences

Combining academic instruction with active application and technical engagement.

Practical Prototyping

Hands-on hardware testing using microcontrollers, digital trainer kits, breadboard layouts, and circuit simulation suites like Proteus.

Technical Competitions

Active involvement in hackathons, logic build-offs, programming workshops, and department-sponsored technology expos.

Industry Internship (OJT)

Direct industry immersion providing practical experience in network operations, firmware engineering, and software development.

Laboratories & Facilities

State-of-the-art laboratory spaces dedicated to hardware, networking, and software research.

Embedded Systems Lab

Equipped with digital logic trainer boards, microprocessors, oscilloscopes, and logic analyzers for hardware circuit design and microcontroller programming.

Cisco Networking Lab

Configured with industry-standard routers, switches, patch panels, and simulation software for enterprise network design and administration.

Robotics & Automation Hub

Specialized workshop space containing sensor modules, actuator components, and fabrication tools for intelligent automation and capstone projects.

Student Projects

  • Smart Automated Irrigation System IoT-based prototype utilizing soil moisture sensors and microcontroller control logic for real-time irrigation management.
  • 8-bit Processor Architecture Simulation Proteus-based simulation model demonstrating instruction processing, register transfers, and bus execution timing.
  • Interactive Embedded Media Controllers Custom hardware-software interface systems integrating real-time input signals with software execution pipelines.

Research & Innovation Activities

The department encourages student and faculty research initiatives focused on emerging technological challenges and practical engineering solutions.

  • IoT & Sensor Networks: Research on wireless sensor deployments and smart environment monitoring.
  • Embedded AI & Automation: Implementation of low-power machine intelligence on edge microcontrollers.
  • Cybersecurity & Networks: System vulnerability assessment and secure routing architecture research.

Academic Opportunities

Graduates and students of the UE Computer Engineering program benefit from professional development pathways, international certification readiness (such as Cisco CCNA), opportunities for research publication, and preparation for technical licensure/certification exams.
